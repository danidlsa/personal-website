Some of my recent work uses social media data, natural language processing and AI models to study gender inequalities in digital spaces: from violence against women in leadership positions to the digital strategies of feminist movements.

-**De los Santos, Daniela, Martin, Guillermina, Urriola, Ivonne & Voria, Andrea** (2025). ‘Entre el menosprecio y las amenazas: análisis de violencia digital contra las mujeres en ocho países de Iberoamérica’. UNDP-LAC – Policy Notes 38. Read it [here](https://www.undp.org/es/latin-america/publicaciones/entre-el-menosprecio-y-las-amenazas-analisis-de-violencia-digital-contra-las-mujeres-en-ocho-paises-de-iberoamerica).

-**Llanos, Beatriz, De los Santos, Daniela & Incio, José** (2023). ‘Mujeres en movimiento(s): el poder del activismo, la incidencia y las estrategias digitales para impulsar transformaciones’. Atenea. UNDP, UN Women and International IDEA. Read the report [here](https://www.undp.org/es/latin-america/publicaciones/mujeres-en-movimientos-el-poder-del-activismo-la-incidencia-y-las-estrategias-digitales-para-la-igualdad-sustantiva).

You can also explore a related exercise, developed during my MSc, that uses NLP models (BERT, LSTM, RNN) to classify stances in tweets about abortion, atheism, feminism and climate: code available [here](https://github.com/danidlsa/nlp-final-tweeteval).
